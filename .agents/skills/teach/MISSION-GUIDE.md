# Mission guide

`MISSION.md` is the compass for what to teach next. Ground it in the user's concrete real-world goal, observable success, constraints, and explicit out-of-scope topics; use [MISSION-FORMAT.md](MISSION-FORMAT.md).

If the mission is missing or unclear, ask why the user wants to learn before designing the lesson. Use existing learning records and the mission to choose a challenge that is just beyond the user's current ability. Do not re-teach established knowledge.

When the user's goal changes, confirm the change, update `MISSION.md`, and record the learning-driven shift in a learning record. Do not let an obsolete mission steer later sessions.
