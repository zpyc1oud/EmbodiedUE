# Fog and scope

The map should be incomplete on purpose. Put a question in **Not yet specified** when it is in scope but cannot yet be phrased precisely. Create a ticket when the question is sharp, even if it is blocked. Do not duplicate a live ticket or a resolved decision in the fog.

Work beyond the destination belongs in **Out of scope**, not in the fog. If an existing ticket turns out to be beyond the destination, close it, record the reason in Out of scope, and keep it out of Decisions so far. If the destination changes, begin a fresh effort.

When a resolution exposes new decisions, create and wire tickets for the questions that are now sharp. Remove their entries from the fog. If a decision invalidates other tickets, update or close those tickets according to the new scope.
